Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

On {{APP_NAME}} the people you meet stay anonymous to each other: they see only a country flag — never your name, email or photo. There are no public profiles and no advertising trackers. This policy explains the data we process and why.

What we process

  • Your account. To keep the Service safe you need an account to chat. We store your name, email address, when you created the account and last signed in, and when you accepted our Community Guidelines. If you sign up with email, we store your password only as a salted one-way hash (scrypt) — never the password itself. If you choose Continue with Google, Google shares your name, email address, Google account ID and a link to your profile picture with us; your Google account is covered by Google’s own privacy policy.
  • IP address. Needed to connect you. We don’t store it in readable form: for moderation we keep only a keyed, one-way hash of it.
  • Approximate country. Worked out from your connection (via our network provider) or your device’s time zone, and shown to the people you chat with as a flag. We don’t use precise location.
  • A random anonymous ID stored in your browser, so reports and bans work and you aren’t matched with the same person over and over. Clearing your browser data removes it.
  • Your preferences (interests, language, settings) are stored only in your browser. Interests and language are sent to our server while you search, to find a match.
  • Text messages are relayed through our server and are not stored — except that when someone reports a chat, up to the last 20 messages of that chat are attached to the report so it can be reviewed.
  • Reports (reason, time, the accounts and anonymous IDs involved and approximate country) are kept as long as needed to review them and enforce our rules.

Video and audio

Your camera and microphone stream goes directly to the person you’re talking to (peer-to-peer). When a direct connection isn’t possible it passes through an encrypted relay (TURN) that cannot see its contents. We do not record, store or watch your video or audio.

Note: in a direct peer-to-peer connection, the other person’s device can technically learn your IP address, as with most video-calling apps.

Cookies

We use two cookies: a sign-in cookie that keeps you signed in (for up to 30 days, or until you sign out), and lang, set only if you choose a language, to remember your choice. Your browser’s local storage holds your preferences and anonymous ID. When the sign-in page offers Google sign-in, your browser loads Google’s sign-in script from Google. We don’t use advertising or analytics cookies.

Sharing

We don’t sell your data. We share information only with providers that run the Service for us (hosting, network, relay servers), or when the law requires it — for example, to report child sexual abuse material to the authorities.

Your rights
