Community Guidelines

Last updated: November 15, 2026

{{APP_NAME}} connects people from every corner of the world. Most conversations are fun, surprising and kind — these rules keep it that way. Breaking them gets you blocked, and serious violations get you banned for good.

The rules

  • Adults only. You must be 18 or older. Report anyone who looks younger.
  • No nudity or sexual content. Keep your clothes on and the conversation non-sexual. No exceptions.
  • No harassment or hate. No insults, threats, bullying, or attacks on anyone’s race, ethnicity, nationality, religion, gender, sexual orientation, disability or appearance.
  • No spam, scams or ads. No links, promotions, selling, begging for money or “add me on…” spam.
  • No violence, self-harm or illegal activity. Don’t show, promote or threaten any of it.
  • No recording without consent. Don’t screenshot, record or stream people who haven’t agreed.
  • Respect “no”. If someone wants to leave or change the topic, let them.

Stay safe

  • Don’t share your full name, address, school, workplace, passwords or financial details.
  • Never send money or gift cards to someone you met here.
  • Be careful moving a conversation to another app — you lose our protections there.
  • You can always press Next (or Esc) to leave a chat instantly.
  • Turn on “Blur new people’s video until I tap” in Settings if you’d like a moment before seeing someone.

Reporting

Tap the flag button during a chat (or shortly after it ends) to report someone. Reports are anonymous, the chat ends immediately, and you won’t be matched with that person again. People who receive multiple reports are blocked automatically while we review.

If someone is in danger

If you believe someone is at immediate risk, contact your local emergency services. To report child exploitation you can also contact the NCMEC CyberTipline or the INHOPE hotline in your country.
